# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

University admissions readers at any university the user applies to. The site is deliberately university-agnostic: no specific university or programme is named, so the same site can accompany every application.

## Product Purpose

A personal, creative portfolio that reflects curiosity about how complex systems work. Personal copy is now filled in from the user's SOP and application portfolio: hero intro, quick facts, three notebook entries, spec sheet and contact email. Sensitive data from the PDF (ID number, phone numbers, address, parent details, date of birth, body measurements) is deliberately excluded. Project sheets and the 'Elsewhere' contact link remain placeholders until the user chooses what to feature.

## Brand Personality

Curious, mechanical, friendly. The experience begins at a slightly messy bedroom study desk: a personal place where questions, repairs and experiments collect. The engineering notebook remains the main visual language; F1 appears as a small personal interest rather than the site's theme.

## Anti-references

Do not invent personal details, present blank project slots as real projects, or assume an F1 team preference beyond the user's explicit request to model the car on the Red Bull RB19 (shape and colours only, no logos). The user has not supplied named visual anti-references.

## Design Principles

- Make the engineering interest tangible through explorable parts and opening notes.
- Keep essential navigation and information available without solving interactions.
- Use only confirmed personal information. Preferred spelling is Rapeepat Sawek, matching the PDF.
- Preserve the paper and dark-panel visual identity already selected.
- Keep the user in control of motion and discoveries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Implementation defaults: keyboard and touch access, visible focus, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. No additional user-specific accessibility needs have been supplied.
